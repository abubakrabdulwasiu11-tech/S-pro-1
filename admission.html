ADMISSIONS

Admission Process

Everything you need to know about applying to Skye-View College for the current Academic Session.

HOW TO APPLY

Step-by-Step Guide

Admission into Skye-View College is open to qualified students. Candidates are admitted based on available spaces and successful completion of the school's admission process.

Candidates may be assessed in relevant subjects including English Language, Mathematics and General Paper.

1

Obtain an Application Form

Obtain an application form from Skye-View College or begin your application through our online application platform.

2

Submit Required Documents

Complete the application form and submit it with the required documents.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

  • Recent passport photographs
  • Photocopy of birth certificate
  • Most recent academic report or result
3

Write the Entrance Examination

Attend the entrance examination on your scheduled date. The examination may cover English Language, Mathematics and General Paper.

4

Oral Interview

Successful candidates may be invited for an oral interview or further assessment after the entrance examination.

5

Receive Your Result

Successful candidates will be informed through the contact details provided during the application process.

6

Make Payment

Complete the required admission and school fee payments through the approved payment channels and proceed with enrollment.
