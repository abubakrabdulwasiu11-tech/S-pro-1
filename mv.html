ABOUT SKYE-VIEW COLLEGE

Our Vision,
Mission & Core Values

The principles and beliefs that guide every decision, every classroom and every learner at Skye-View College.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution with the utmost concern of raising highly competent and God-fearing transformational leader for future

Our Mission

We are committed to assuring that each child comes into confident possession of his innate talents, improves the skills needed for success in school, and establishes values that will allow him act with thoughtfulness and humanity.

Our Core Values

At Skye-View College, our identity is built on five enduring values represented by the acronym PFRUC. These principles shape our culture, guide our decisions, and inspire every student to become a person of integrity, excellence, and positive impact.
