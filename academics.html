HOW WE LEARN

Committed to Learning Excellence

At Skye-View College, we believe that education should build knowledge, character, confidence and the skills students need to succeed in a changing world.

Our curriculum combines strong academic teaching with practical learning, creativity, technology and activities that encourage every student to discover and develop their potential.

01
JUNIOR SECONDARY SCHOOL

Junior Secondary Years

Our Junior Secondary School programme provides students with a strong academic foundation while helping them develop confidence, discipline, curiosity and essential learning skills.

Students are exposed to a broad range of subjects that prepare them for future academic choices and the transition into Senior Secondary School.

KEY LEARNING AREAS

English Language Mathematics Basic Science Basic Technology Social Studies Business Studies Computer Studies Creative Arts Physical Education Argric Science Yoruba Islamic Religious Studies
02
SENIOR SECONDARY SCHOOL

Senior Secondary Years

At the Senior Secondary level, students build deeper knowledge in their chosen academic areas while preparing for external examinations and future educational opportunities.

Our teachers support students through structured learning, academic guidance, practical activities and regular assessment.

KEY LEARNING AREAS

English Language Mathematics Civic Education Digital Technology Economics Further Mathematics Biology Chemistry Physics Government Literature-in-English Financial Accounting
03
ACADEMIC DEPARTMENT

Science & Innovation

Our Science programme encourages students to explore, question, investigate and develop a strong understanding of the world through scientific learning and practical experience.

SCIENCE SUBJECTS

Biology Chemistry Physics Mathematics Further Mathematics Digital Technology
04
ACADEMIC DEPARTMENT

Arts & Commercial Studies

Students in our Arts and Commercial programmes develop strong communication, analytical, creative and problem-solving skills for further education and future careers.

KEY SUBJECTS

Government Literature-in-English Fashion Garment Making Islamic Religious Studies Financial Accounting Commerce Marketing
05
FUTURE-READY LEARNING

Technology & Practical Learning

We believe students should be prepared for a world driven by technology, innovation and new ideas. Our learning approach encourages creativity, digital literacy and practical skills.

LEARNING EXPERIENCES

Computer Studies Digital Skills Programming Creative Projects Practical Technology Innovation
